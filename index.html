DEPED CURRICULUMS

The Department of Education's (DepEd) current basic education framework is the MATATAG Curriculum, a streamlined and decongested version of the K to 10 program designed to focus heavily on foundational skills.

Junior High School (JHS)

Junior High School an intermediate educational level that bridges elementary school and senior high school.

Senior High School (SHS)

Senior High School, which covers Grades 11 and 12 (the final two years of basic education before college, technical training, or work).

Special Curricular Program (SCPs)

Special Curricular Programs (SCPs) are specialized elective offerings designed to nurture students' unique talents, multiple intelligence, and career pathways within the K-12 basic education system.
